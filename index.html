🚀 System Overview & Modules

The system combines empirical agronomic science with Scikit-learn Machine Learning pipelines to assist farmers, students, and agricultural researchers in maximizing yield while safeguarding soil sustainability.

🌱

Soil Health Analysis

Evaluates primary macronutrients and moisture, classifies fertility status into Healthy, Moderate, or Needs Improvement, and suggests corrective fertilizers.

🌾

Crop Recommendation

Predicts the optimal crop variety among 22 options based on local soil nutrients and microclimate with probability confidence and agronomic reasoning.

📊

Yield & Revenue Forecast

Estimates production in Tonnes/Ha, Total Harvest (Quintals), and gross revenue in INR and USD using official Minimum Support Price benchmarks.

💡 Agronomic Harvest Optimization

  • Split Nitrogen Application: Apply 50% as basal dose, 25% during tillering, and 25% at panicle emergence to curb leaching.
  • Moisture Critical Window: Maintain saturation during flowering to avoid grain sterility and achieve maximum harvest.
  • Mandi Valuation: Calculated using Government Minimum Support Price (MSP) benchmark.

ℹ️ About the Academic Mini Project

Smart Agriculture System for Soil Health Analysis, Crop Recommendation and Yield Prediction

Submitted in partial fulfillment of the academic requirements for Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) / Master of Computer Applications (MCA) in Computer Science & Engineering / Information Technology.

Key Project Contributions:
  • Multi-objective agricultural platform integrating soil health, crop classification, and yield regression.
  • Non-blackbox agronomic suitability justifications for 22 crops.
  • Monetary revenue modeling based on Government Minimum Support Price (MSP) benchmarks.
  • Dual deployment: Interactive Streamlit application and zero-dependency standalone HTML frontend.
Technology Stack: Python 3.11 • Streamlit • Scikit-learn • Pandas • Plotly • HTML5/CSS3 • SQLite
